# I. What Not To Post

1. Unverified health claims, treatment advice, cure claims or before-and-after patient outcome promises.
2. Direct consumer promotions of prescription medicines, medicine pricing posts or product-use recommendations.
3. Patient faces, names, records, hospital documents or home-care situations without documented consent.
4. Claims that an aquaculture project guarantees yield, profit, approvals or investment returns.
5. A completed `PhD` claim before the doctorate is awarded; state `Doctoral Researcher` and `Expected completion: 2027`.
6. Generic motivational quote graphics that dilute the premium, evidence-led identity.
7. Excessive selfies, personal lifestyle updates or unrelated celebrations unless they carry clear professional/community meaning.
8. Unrelated political, religiously divisive or argumentative content on the professional account.
9. Low-quality stock photos, cluttered poster creatives or daily sales flyers.
10. Testimonials, institutional logos, student images or partner branding without permission.
